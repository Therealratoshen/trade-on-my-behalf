## Stage 1 — Empathize

### Persona A — "The Founder" (primary)

> Solo retail trader. Crypto-native since 2021. Has held SOL through
> two cycles. Recognizes setups in seconds. Cannot sit in front of
> charts because they run a non-crypto job.
>
> **Evidence status: founder-as-customer hypothesis, partially validated.**
> The "missed setup because I was at work" anchor is the most
> under-validated claim in the empathy map — flag for D11.

#### Says

- *(evidence, founder chat history 2026-09-26)*
  > "i do not have time to do trading if the action or trend or so
  > called the movement can be predictable and i should trade it but
  > i do not have time to review and keep on viewing it at all"
- *(evidence, founder chat history 2026-09-26)*
  > "what i really want to have is the perpetual agent to trade for
  > me"
- *(external validation, TradingView setup guide)* General "a messed
  up chart means missed trades" — the missed-setup pain has a
  non-crypto audience analog.
- *(assumption)* "Set a stop, walk away. That's the whole game."
- *(assumption)* "If the bot oversizes once, I'm out forever."
- *(assumption)* "I don't want a dashboard. I want a Telegram ping."

#### Thinks

- *(assumption)* "The setup is *right there* — I can see it. I just
  can't be there when it fires."
- *(external validation, r/Daytrading "Blew up my account")* Trader
  blowups frequently happen because size was raised during life stress
  — *"My troubles began when I felt my job security waver due to
  advancements in AI, prompting me to increase my trade sizes."*
- *(assumption)* "If I let a 24/7 bot run it, what stops a bad signal
  from blowing the account?"
- *(assumption)* "Every other perps bot says 'AI agent.' They all
  sound the same. Which one *can't* break my rules?"
- *(assumption)* "If I had a system I trusted, I'd let it size for
  me. Right now I don't trust any of them."

#### Does

- *(evidence)* Wrote SPEC, wedge verdict, dimension map, treasury
  program. Owns the problem. (PM-LOG D1–D8.)
- *(assumption)* Pulls up charts on phone 5–10× per day for ~30 s,
  sees setup, runs out of time before executing.
- *(assumption)* Subscribed to 2–3 paid TG signal channels in the
  past 12 months. Copy-traded one, lost money, stopped.
- *(assumption)* Holds positions on Jupiter Perps + Drift manually
  with mental caps ($200 max, 15% down → walk away). Caps are
  willpower, not code.
- *(assumption)* Wakes up to missed setups *more often* than to bad
  fills. *(Test on D11.)*

#### Feels

| Pain (now) | Validation | Mirrored gain (desired) | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frustration** — setups visible but unreachable | ⚠️ weak | **Joy** — setups taken on time | ⚠️ weak |
| **Distrust** — every 24/7 bot could oversize | ✅ strong (Maestro, Unibot, 3Commas exploits; "$1.1M lost" / "$14.8M across 44 victims") | **Trust** — runtime *cannot* break rules | ✅ strong |
| **Anxiety** — unattended bot while I sleep | ✅ strong (r/Daytrading blowup stories; Satsdats: "oversizing is the root cause of virtually every trading mistake") | **Anticipation** — next DM = good trade, never a leak | ⚠️ mixed |
| (sec.) **Sadness** — missed moves | ⚠️ weak | **Surprise** — wins I didn't have to be there for | ⚠️ weak |
| (new) **Speed-pressure anxiety** — 60s TG DM could *cause* FOMO | ⚠️ weak | (mirrored) **Calm** — timeout acts as a circuit breaker, not a pressure | ⚠️ weak |

> Pain-row anchor remains Plutchik **distrust** (the cognitively-loaded
> sibling of fear). Mirrored gain is **trust**. The wedge line —
> *"I trade for you, and I cannot break your rules"* — delivers that
> shift.

### Persona B — "The Prop-Trading Firm Operator" (adjacent, **now validated**)

> Runs a 3–8-person desk on Solana. Wallet-per-strategy bookkeeping

  trade, and the *rules* to live somewhere I can audit."

  uses.

#### Thinks

- *(external validation, defiprime "Onchain Prop Firms")* *"The
  onchain version takes the three things prop firms have historically
  been able to fudge — the rulebook, the payouts, and the counterparty

  published form.
- *(assumption)* "Compliance is going to ask for this in 12 months
  anyway."
- *(assumption)* "Off-chain rule engines are a soft target. Give me
  something signed."
- *(assumption)* "If the policy lives in the wallet's PDA, I can hand
  the same wallet to a junior and not worry."

#### Does

- *(external validation, hyrotrader blog)* Real prop firms currently
  *"use a Google Sheet + manual checks at end-of-day. Misses intraday
  breaches."* — the persona's workflow is the default.
- *(external validation, hyrotrader blog)* Real firms have
  *"tried a vendor's 'risk engine' once; the vendor goes offline twice
  in a quarter; the desk goes back to the spreadsheet."* — the
  persona's reason for wanting on-chain.
- *(assumption)* Audited once in 2025; the auditor asked for a
  per-trade decision trail with inputs; the desk produced a CSV
  hand-built by the founder. Painful.

#### Feels

| Pain (now) | Validation | Mirrored gain (desired) | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distrust** — off-chain risk engines | ✅ strong (HyroTrader article: vendor goes offline twice in a quarter) | **Trust** — on-chain policy cannot be bypassed | ✅ strong |
| **Anxiety** — weekends / after-hours | ✅ strong | **Anticipation** — kill-switch fires before damage | ✅ strong (Propr rulebook pinned in contract) |
| **Disgust** — manual spreadsheet reconciliation | ✅ strong | **Joy** — 1-click on-chain audit export | ✅ strong |
| (sec.) **Anger** — junior strategy overshot | ✅ strong (r/Daytrading blowup stories) | **Surprise** — denied trade surfaces *before* it hurts | ✅ strong |

---

### Synthesis (across A + B + C)

- **The dominant pain emotion across personas is fear-family**
  (distrust, anxiety) with secondary frustration-family.
- **The mirrored gain is trust-family**, achieved through
  *demonstrable* constraints the user can feel happening — TG
  auto-skip, dashboard deny banner, on-chain `AuditEvent` for every
  decision.
- **The empathy-map rule holds:** we read pains from expressed
> feelings, not stated problems. The persona's words are about
> time, custody, and size; their *feelings* are about trust and
> oversight. The product must deliver the feelings.

## Stage 2 — Define

### POV problem statements

#### Persona A — The Founder

> The founder needs a perps execution path that takes their
> personally-defined setups *only when they cannot be at the screen*,
> because they recognize good trades in seconds but lose hours to
> waiting and miss more setups than they catch, but currently every
> 24/7 bot they evaluate either oversizes on a bad signal (Satsdats,
> r/Daytrading blowup stories), hides the decision off-chain (Maestro,
> Unibot, 3Commas exploits), or asks them to keep watching.

#### Persona B — The Prop-Firm Operator

> The prop-trading operator needs a per-strategy wallet that refuses
> to act outside pre-declared on-chain rules — distinct from the
> firm-level rulebooks Propr / HyroTrader / Solana Funded /
> DojiFunded already ship — because a single unmonitored oversize has
> already wiped out a strategy twice and the auditor is asking for a
> per-trade decision trail, but currently the rules live in a
> spreadsheet that cannot refuse a trade and an off-chain risk engine
> that has gone offline twice.

#### Persona C — The Influencer-Signal Follower

> The influencer-signal follower needs a copy-trading wallet that
> clamps the influencer's signal to a *per-follower* hard cap
> (per-day size, daily-loss cap, drawdown kill), because the
> influencer's signal can call sizing that the follower's bankroll
> can't survive, but currently every copy-trading bot exposes the
> follower's full bankroll to the influencer's full intent.

### Raw ideas (14) — clustered by HMW

#### Cluster — HMW-A4 (TG approve/deny)

5. **TG Bot API long-poll + inline `Approve`/`Deny` buttons** —
   60-second TTL with `Promise.race` auto-skip. ✅ canonical answer;

6. *(assumption)* Telegram Mini-App as rules editor + approve

7. *(assumption)* SMS approve via Twilio. Wrong UX, expensive, not

#### Cluster — HMW-A5 (RiskFlag preview)

8. **Single combined DM: trade-proposal + RiskFlag reason inline** —
   one DM per intent, reason-code rendered in the message body.
   ✅ canonical answer.
9. *(assumption)* Separate RiskFlag channel vs trade-proposal
   channel. Reduces noise but breaks the "every decision visible in
   one place" feel. Drop.
10. *(assumption)* RiskFlag only on deny (not on timeout/approve).
    Quieter, but loses the "rules just fired" feeling moment. Drop.

#### Cross-cluster (catches two HMWs at once)

11. **`RiskFlag` DM auto-fires on any deny — and the trade-proposal
    DM shows a *previewed* reason code if it *would* deny.** This is
    ideas 5 + 8 + 10 fused: every intent gets a DM with the would-be
    outcome already filled in. ✅ canonical answer; elevates the
    feeling shift.
12. *(assumption)* After-action DM at close. Strong but v2.

#### Wild-cards

13. *(assumption)* "Demo mode" — a fake TG chat visible on the

### The idea we ship

> **The combined "Trade On My Behalf" on-chain policy plane** — a
> single end-user perps product where:
>
> 1. every intent is a DM with the trade proposal **and** the
>    would-be rule outcome already filled in,
> 2. the on-chain Anchor `Policy` PDA is the *only* signing path
>    (no off-chain bypass possible),
> 3. every approved and denied decision emits an `AuditEvent`,
>    surfaced as a `RiskFlag` DM on deny and as a dashboard row on
>    approve,
> 4. a 60-second auto-skip timeout protects the founder from
>    unreachable-phone scenarios,
> 5. the same kernel also serves prop-firm operators (per-strategy
>    wallet with hard caps, distinct from Propr's firm-level
>    rulebook) and influencer-signal followers (per-follower cap
>    on the influencer's intent).
>
> This is the SPEC.md v1-c9 / D3' shape. The ideation pass confirms
> the SPEC matches the wedge, validates the three HMWs against
> external evidence, and adds the speed-pressure risk to the
> Stage 5 test rubric.

### 1-line DFV defense per criterion

- **Desirability** — delivers the three after-feelings (Trust via
  the on-chain gate firing; Joy via the 60s approve-or-skip
  *contingent on D11 outcome*; Anticipation via the `RiskFlag`
  previewed in the proposal DM) that the empathy map's Persona A row
  says the founder is buying.
- **Feasibility** — the on-chain kernel compiles D4, extended D7-D8
  with `update_policy`, `record_pnl`, and on-chain drawdown kill;
  the TG bot is already specced in `docs/control-surface.md`; venue
  adapters are 200 LOC each (per SPEC §"Tech stack");
  `scripts/devnet-demo.sh` reproduces end-to-end by D10.
- **Viability** — 5/6 judging criteria are met by this shape (Tech:
  kernel compiles + extends; Biz: performance-fee pitch; Novelty:
  P+A+X+T+M empty in `docs/research/dimension-map.md`; UX: TG DM
  primary; OSS: MIT across the board). The sixth (composability) is
  delivered by Jupiter Perps + Drift + Helius + AgentBazaar as
  primitives. Prop-firm segment validates Persona B (4 live firms);
  influencer-signal follower validates Persona C via copy-trading
  market analog.

### Riskiest assumption

> **Two risks tied for first place.**
>
> 1. **The on-chain gate must demonstrably deny in the demo.**
>    If the pitch video's *"rules just fired"* scene is mocked or
>    hand-waved, judges will down-score Technical and Novelty both.
>    **Mitigation:** the demo script pre-stages a rule that *will*
>    deny (e.g. `maxLeverage: 1`, trade proposed at 5x), so the
>    `RiskFlag` DM is observed in real time, not narrated.
>
> 2. **The 60s TG DM timeout must feel empowering, not pressuring.**
>    The published bot-safety literature emphasizes FOMO as a harm;
>    a 60s "tap or skip" loop could *cause* Anxiety rather than
>    relieve it. **Mitigation:** Stage 5 testers on D11 measure the
>    feeling-shift explicitly via the semantic-pair delta from
>    `05-test.md`. If 2 of 3 testers describe the timeout as
>    pressuring, the v1.5 patch adds configurable TTL (per `BRD
>    review` action item).
